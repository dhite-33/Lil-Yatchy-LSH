Highlight Tracks

1) "the BLACK seminole": This track is praised for its incredible production, with some comparisons to Pink Floyd's "The Dark Side of the Moon," the song sets a tone that can excite and prepare the listener for the ride ahead.

 

2) "the ride-": This track follows the intro with another outstanding performance by Lil Yatchy and upcoming star Teezo Touchdown.

 

3) "drive ME crazy!": This track is considered the album's funkiest and the pop stand out, featuring a hypnotic blend of groovy basslines and rhythmic synths.
