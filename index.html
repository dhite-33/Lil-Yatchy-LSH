Yatchy has a new Style??

American rapper, singer, and songwriter from Atlanta, Georgia, Lil Yachty — known for hit songs such as "Minnesota" and "Flex Up" — reaches far beyond the norm to create this psychedelic piece of work that some say is revolutionizing modern hip-hop and music in general. "Let's Start Here." is a polished, complete album that transports you to distant places in your mind with shifting instrumentals and unique vocal performances.

Yachty's Evalution

"Let's Start Here." marks a major shift for Lil Yachty, moving away from his earlier "bubblegum trap" and mumble rap roots toward a sound blending alternative rock, soul, and touches of metal. The album has received critical acclaim, helping transform Yachty’s image from a frequent target of criticism to a respected artist in the rap world. Reflecting his personal growth and artistic evolution, Yachty’s willingness to explore new genres has earned recognition from industry peers, including collaborations like his work on Drake’s Her Loss. The album’s success has sparked anticipation for his future projects, with fans and critics expecting continued innovation.
